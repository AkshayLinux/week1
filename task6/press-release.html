What is FiPlate t1

Ever wondered why your laptop sound like a jet engine? Well, you may know the beast inside it is a heat machine while you use it. Everyone was searching for cooler chips and bigger fan blades, but we know something else is the answer. Thus we planned to build the revolutionary FiPlate t1 series. The device we made is capable of pushing 10 times the air a regular laptop fan can push with 3 times less space for installation. This seems unreal but this is pure science. The device by dimension is 5cmx5cmx0.5cm but can push air faster than a regular 8 blade PC fan. Most intresting part is FiPlate didn't have any moving parts and thus it produce no noise at all just pure air, with pure science

Where we master?

We mainly aim electronic devices where cooling is required more than ever before and space for installation is smaller than any other product. We're already in partnership with tech giants like MSI, GigaByte, and popular motherboard manufacturers.

Science behind

We use ionized air technology to push air at 30 miles per hour in a .5 cm chamber. The main power converter here merely uses 5v DC, though it can pull higher amps on cooler high room remperatures.
